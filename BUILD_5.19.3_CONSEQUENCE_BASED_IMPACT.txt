Build 5.19.3 — Consequence-based ISRO impact classification (no LLM)

Changes:
- Removed single-keyword High Impact behavior.
- concern_ids no longer force High Impact by themselves.
- High now requires direct institutional relevance plus severe/material consequences, or multiple consequence dimensions.
- Evaluates mission/safety, schedule, resources, strategic/regulatory, institutional and ecosystem consequences separately.
- Generic mentions of launch, contract, policy, approval, regulation or delay do not automatically become High.
- Private-sector growth, funding, manufacturing and partnerships normally classify Medium unless paired with a direct severe consequence.
- Minor delays can remain Low when no wider consequence is evident; major mission-linked delays can be High.
- Tone remains separate from impact.
- No LLM or external AI API is used.
