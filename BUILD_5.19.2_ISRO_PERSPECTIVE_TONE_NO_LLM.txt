BUILD 5.19.2 — ISRO-PERSPECTIVE TONE CLASSIFICATION (NO LLM)
============================================================

Purpose
-------
Make the scheduled news report's Positive / Negative / Neutral field reflect the
meaning of the article from the perspective of ISRO, Department of Space (DoS),
IN-SPACe and NSIL.

Key change
----------
The old generic VADER/negative-keyword rule in scheduled_media_reports.py has
been replaced for scheduled reports by a local, deterministic stance classifier.

NEGATIVE
--------
Negative is used when coverage is directed against ISRO/DoS/IN-SPACe/NSIL and is
critical, derogatory, accusatory, disparaging or reputation-damaging, including
material questioning of competence, credibility, integrity, governance,
performance or decisions.

POSITIVE
--------
Positive is used for direct praise/recognition, successful achievements, and
clearly beneficial developments for the ISRO/Indian-space ecosystem such as
successful missions, useful cooperation, technology transfer and meaningful
private-space ecosystem growth.

NEUTRAL
-------
Neutral is used for factual/balanced coverage without a clear favourable or
reputation-damaging stance.

Important separation
--------------------
Bad event != Negative tone.
Examples: weather postponement, technical anomaly, delay, accident or failure do
not automatically become Negative. They can remain Neutral in tone while still
receiving Medium/High operational impact.

Direction matters
-----------------
"ISRO was criticised ..." -> Negative
"Experts criticised ISRO ..." -> Negative
"ISRO criticised a supplier ..." -> Neutral unless the story also attacks ISRO
"ISRO launch delayed by weather" -> Neutral
"ISRO successfully completes test" -> Positive
"Indian private space ecosystem expands" -> Positive when tied to ISRO/DoS/
IN-SPACe/NSIL and clearly beneficial to the domestic ecosystem.

Architecture
------------
No LLM, OpenAI call, or API key is used. The classifier uses directional stance
patterns, institutional anchoring, event combinations and headline weighting.
Impact Level and Impact Analysis remain separate fields.
